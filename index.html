Approach: Landing Simulator

Tilt to fly. Famous airports. Every flight starts forty seconds from the runway, and the only thing you have to do is put the aeroplane down well.

Thirty-two flights, four aircraft, and ten real approaches built from the real charts — Lukla, Courchevel, Saba, St Barthélemy, Toncontín, Madeira, Gibraltar, St Maarten, Barra, Paro — plus Kai Tak, Sydney and London City. Fifteen flights are free; one purchase opens the rest. No ads, no accounts, no data collected.
