Approach: Landing Simulator

Support

Version 1.0 · iPhone and iPad · iOS 16 and later

Every flight starts forty seconds from the runway, and the only thing you have to do is put the aeroplane down well. Thirty-two flights, four aircraft, and ten real approaches built to their true runway lengths.

The controls

Hold the device in landscape, in two hands, like a small control column.

  • TiltThe stick. Lean the device left and the aircraft banks left; bring the top edge toward you and the nose comes up. It holds whatever attitude you set — nothing levels itself.
  • Left leverThrottle. It sets your speed.
  • Right leverFlaps, one notch at a time. Flaps let the wing fly slower without stalling.
  • Bottom cornersRudder pedals — for kicking straight in a crosswind, and for steering on the rollout.
  • GearYou start clean, so the wheels are yours to lower. Three seconds to lock down.
  • BrakeHold it. On the jets, Rev is reverse thrust.
  • Go aroundAbandon the approach and try again — on every field except Lukla, which has no go-around, exactly as in life.

If the aircraft keeps rolling on its own

Tap the artificial horizon dial — the round instrument at the bottom centre of the screen. That re-zeros the tilt neutral to however you happen to be holding the device at that moment.

This is the answer to almost every "the tilt feels wrong" problem. Your grip drifts as you play, you shift on the sofa, you cross your legs — tap the dial and the aeroplane is level again where your hands are now. You can do it at any time, including on short final.

If the tilt feels twitchy or heavy in general rather than off-centre, that is a different fix: on the home screen tap Settings → Tilt sensitivity and try Gentle (the least movement needed) or Sharp (the most responsive). Standard is the default and asks for about 30° of lean for full bank.

Restoring a purchase

If you have bought "Unlock All Levels" and reinstalled the app, changed device, or the levels look locked again:

  1. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.
  2. Open the app, and on the home screen tap Settings.
  3. Under Purchases, tap Restore purchases.

The same button is also on the purchase sheet itself. Restoring is free and can be done as often as you like. If it says no purchase was found, check which Apple ID the device is signed in to — that is the cause nine times out of ten. If it still will not restore, email me and I will sort it out.

If it does not run smoothly

On the home screen tap Settings → Graphics and choose Balanced or Smooth. Detailed is the default and is what the game is meant to look like; Smooth turns off the most expensive effects and is there for older hardware.

Questions

Is this a real flight simulator?

No, and it is not trying to be. It is an arcade game about the last forty seconds of a flight. There is no flight planning, no cockpit to cold-start, no air traffic control, no systems to manage. The physics is tuned to feel right in your hands rather than to match a certified aircraft, and it will not teach you to fly a real aeroplane.

Are the airports real?

Ten of them are. Paro, Lukla, Courchevel, Saba, St Barthélemy, Toncontín, Madeira, Gibraltar, Princess Juliana and Barra are built from published runway dimensions and approach geometry — every length, width and gradient is the real one, converted and not rounded to make anything easier. Kai Tak is recreated too. The aircraft, by contrast, are invented: four fictional classes, not modelled on or named after any manufacturer's product.

What do I get for free, and what does the purchase open?

Fifteen of the thirty-two flights are free: the whole first chapter — which includes five of the ten real airports — plus the first two flights of each of the other three chapters. One purchase opens the remaining seventeen and the other three aircraft. It is a single one-off purchase. There is no subscription, no in-game currency, no advertising and no energy timer.

Do you collect any data about me?

No. There is no networking code in the app at all — no account, no analytics, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere. Your progress lives on your device. See the privacy policy.

I keep crashing into the runway. What am I doing wrong?

Usually one of three things. You are arriving fast — pull the throttle to idle before the threshold and let the speed come off. You are not flaring — at the "10" callout, tilt the top of the device toward you briefly, then ease back and let it settle; don't hold the pull, or you will float the whole runway. Or the gear is still up: you start clean on every flight, so lowering it is your job.

Can I play it on an iPhone?

Yes. iPhone and iPad, landscape, iOS 16 and later. It is the same game on both.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to make the one purchase. Everything else — all thirty-two flights — works with the device in aeroplane mode, which is the state a game about landing aeroplanes ought to work in.
