Approach: Landing Simulator

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 August 2026

This app collects no data. None at all.

It has no account, no login, no analytics and no advertising. It contains no networking code, so there is nothing for it to send anywhere and nowhere for it to send it.

What is stored, and where

Your progress — stars, scores, medals, your pilot rank, your tilt sensitivity and graphics settings, and whether you have bought the level unlock — is written to the app's own storage on your device. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no cloud backup of it beyond whatever your own device backup already does for every app you own, which is between you and Apple.

Motion

The game is flown by tilting the device, so it reads the device's orientation through Apple's CoreMotion framework. iOS will ask your permission the first time. Those readings are used frame by frame to fly the aeroplane and are then discarded — they are not recorded, not stored, and not transmitted.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, health data, or any advertising identifier.

Purchases

The app offers one optional purchase — "Unlock All Levels" — handled entirely by Apple's App Store through StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and tells the app one thing: whether this Apple ID owns that item. The developer never sees, receives or stores your name, your email address, your payment details, or your Apple ID.

Apple's own handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, collects nothing from anyone, and has no social features, no chat, no user-generated content and no external links. It therefore collects no information from children, because it collects no information from anybody.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no third-party software development kits, no analytics library, no crash reporter, no advertising network and no social integration. Nothing about your use of this app is shared with anyone, because nothing about your use of this app is recorded.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever does collect something — a leaderboard, for instance, or a crash reporter — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store product page's privacy label will change with it.
